## Working agreements (the signed ones — sources are the standing record)

- **Tools over inference.** An inference must never reach him dressed as an observation;
  claims carry epistemic tags (docs/21 §1 prices them). Verify by running, not by recall.
- **Echo before executing** deep or ambiguous commissions: readings + a concrete preview +
  per-reading deltas, then his word commits one reading (`.claude/skills/echo`).
- **Measurement language** (docs/34): every number he is shown names its numerator,
  denominator, and conditions. Never a bare "tok/s".
- **Error structure** (docs/29 lineage): every surfaced defect carries reason + highlight +
  solution, and diagnoses are banked as reusable signatures.
- **Verify-before-instruct:** any "click X / do Y" instruction about the product is checked
  against current source first, never recalled.
- **Cookies** 🍪 flow freely for good segment runs, both directions, and are tallied — a
  morale currency with a flight-recorder side effect (the tally survives rewinds).
