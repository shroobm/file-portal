There are eight transducers in our basic diagram. These examples are all actual cases of inadequate variety in transduction — given that basic variety (First Principle) is capable of absorption, and channel capacity (Second Principle) can convey that variety.

**OPERATIONS**

- Government health dept: The phrase ‘loyal support’, encoded in an instruction from management was ambiguous because objectives under debate.
- Mammal corporation: glossy annual reports, issued to workers with pay packets, discarded at pay-out counter unopened.
- Nationalized industry: placards it workers’ demonstration encoded in terms of a different issue than that under protest.
