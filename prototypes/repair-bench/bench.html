The Repair Bench — how it works

What this is

The audit can say where a conversion went wrong — it cannot see what the page really held. You can. The bench puts the source page and the damaged markdown side by side so a repair is one gesture. Click the ◆ icon any time to put a different book on the bench — held bundles, pending bundles, anchor copies, or a bare PDF (which opens in honest reading mode: browse, search, no writes).

The three panes

Left: Contents is the PDF's own outline · Search finds text in the book (raw scans honestly say they have no text layer; results never yank you away — you click when ready) · Pages is a thumbnail rail that opens at your page.

Middle: the source page. ⌖ locate finds a zone's true page from the prose around the damage on text-layer books; raw scans get a labeled ~ratio guess your eyes refine.

Right: the markdown at the zone — the ▶ line is the wreck; your repairs show green.

Starting a repair (the loop the ☰ coach walks with you)

1 · Pick a zone — the chips are the audit's flagged wreck sites (hover one for its excerpt). 2 · Find the page — ◂ ▸ and zoom until you SEE the damage. 3 · Capture — drag a rectangle → ✂ insert crop, or Ctrl+V a screenshot; it lands in assets/, embedded at the zone, provenance stamped. 4 · Fix the text — the AI bar (✦ fix / ↩ undo) or ✎ edit; this is what clears the degeneration flag. 5 · ◎ re-score — an honest preview; audit credit is Rab's unsigned policy.

The AI's rules

Local qwen3 only; sees only the visible passage; every image embed is fenced into an opaque token first and a damaged fence means the change is refused; "nothing to fix" is honest and costs no undo; ↩ undo AI is byte-identical, twenty deep.

Reading aids (S112, from the Okular digest)

Your place is saved per book — under the source's own id, so a pipeline rewrite can't lose it — and restored when the book returns to the bench. ↶ ↷ (Alt+←/→) walk your jump history: page changes push an entry, plain scrolling never does. Marks (sidebar): ★ saves the exact page + scroll; double-click renames; ✕ deletes. ⛶ trim cuts the margins, measured from the page raster (+4% pad, never past half the page); Shift+⛶ then drag sets your own trim box for every page; Esc cancels. Page turns paint the low-dpi raster after 120 ms instead of leaving you a blank wait — fast pages skip it entirely.

Source evidence (OK-15 quarantine)

◉ evidence measures MuPDF warnings, logical page labels, independent reading-order differences, an all-layers-off raster counterfactual, and embedded PDF thumbnail metadata. Click any listed page to inspect it here. UNREAD reasons are shown rather than counted as clean zeroes. Failed collections are remembered so a broken book cannot charge you repeatedly; use ↻ retry collection when you deliberately want another attempt. A second request says IN-PROGRESS instead of silently waiting. This report stays in memory: it does not change the PDF, conversion, bundle, audit verdict, or pipeline.

Your safety net

.md.bench-bak before the first write · append-only provenance in manifest["repairs"] · the SANDBOX badge means you're on a copy · the READING badge means nothing can write at all · and the pipeline can never overwrite a bundle that carries your repairs.
