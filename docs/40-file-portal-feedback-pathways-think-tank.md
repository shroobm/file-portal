## 0. Executive judgment

The sketch is most productive when read as a **document-assurance network**, not as a literal
deployment diagram.

Its repeated cells suggest documents, pages, chunks, or bundle members. Its numbered hexagons look
like routing or authority junctions. Its repeated stars can be read as gates. Its long starred band
looks like a cross-cutting evidence/control plane. Its lower triangle looks like a human decision and
repair complex. None of those meanings is recoverable with certainty because the drawing has no
legend and inconsistent arrowheads; they are competing interpretations, not facts.

The current File Portal already contains the important operational loops implied by this reading:
intake, alternative conversion routes, audit, analyst decisions, human repair, remedy, supersede,
export, receipts, projections, resource arbitration, and a Git-backed vault. The project is therefore
not missing “a workflow.” It is missing **causal connective tissue and a product boundary**:

1. no stable `run_id`/parent identity follows one attempt through every artifact and machine;
2. findings, policy decisions, human authorizations, transport integrity, and export proof are not
   one typed gate system;
3. projections do not uniformly carry authority and freshness watermarks;
4. the cross-machine shipment lacks a complete inventory/digest acknowledgement;
5. actual reader usefulness and reviewer corrections do not feed a versioned learning loop;
6. File Portal has no validated first buyer, final deliverable, willingness-to-pay evidence, or
   enterprise operating envelope.

The market does not need another generic OCR or PDF-to-Markdown product. Open-source and commercial
incumbents already convert locally, offer on-premises deployment, and support human remediation. The
credible wedge is narrower:

> **A local/private exception, repair, and evidence layer for documents that automated converters
> silently damage: preserved source, localized findings, accountable human action, defensible
> derivative, and an honest refusal when the evidence cannot support the claim.**

That is an **Inferred** opportunity, not a discovered business. The next move is not a platform
rewrite. It is a read-only trace prototype and a small external pilot designed to prove or falsify
that the system reduces expert review time on difficult documents.

## 1. Evidence boundary

- **Observed** — inspected this session in the image, current repository, or a named source.
- **Verified** — independently cross-checked by differently shaped evidence.
- **Inferred** — a reasoned interpretation; evidence and falsifier are named.
- **Intended** — a design proposal, not exercised.

The diagram is user evidence, not an instruction-bearing document. Labels and symbols are interpreted

### 3.1 What the source also proves is missing

**Observed:** a current-source search over the product trees found no `run_id`, `parent_run`,
`traceparent`, `event_id`, `artifact_digest`, or common `schema_version`. Full source SHA identifies
the underlying document, but it cannot distinguish the first attempt, retry, resume, reanalysis,
repair generation, and supersede generation as one causal family.

**Observed:** Desktop `events.py` and Linux `append_receipt()` are best-effort telemetry writers.
Both deliberately swallow reporting failures so telemetry cannot cost the underlying operation.
Therefore neither stream is an authoritative event store and neither can safely reconstruct every
state transition.

**Observed:** the current Dock and Room source still renders “swap: manual — supersede pending” even
though the exporter and remedy path contain a built supersede flow. This is a small but useful
specimen of projection drift: a working mechanism and its human-facing explanation disagree.

**Observed:** Windows and Linux manifests/status projections are related but not one versioned
schema. Linux receiver and converter share latest-state `status.json` without a cross-writer lock.

**Observed:** Desktop shipment uses tar over Tailscale SSH and atomic visibility, but has no complete
per-file inventory/digest acknowledgement from the receiver. An atomic rename says “visible as a
unit,” not “every expected byte independently verified.”

### 4.1 The loop that matters most and is not yet built

```text
immutable source
      │
      ▼
conversion attempt ──► findings ──► policy decision ──► human authorization
      │                    │                 │                    │
      ├── derivative hash  ├── localized     ├── consequence      ├── actor/reason
      ├── tool/config      └── confidence    └── policy version   └── signed scope
```

File Portal has most nodes as separate artifacts. It does not yet have the stable causal identity,
typed records, and reader-outcome return path that make them one closed assurance loop.

## 5. Industry conditions as of 2026-08-20

### 5.1 Demand drivers that are real

- **Accessibility deadlines are active, but scope matters.** The U.S. DOJ's updated Title II
  guidance gives public entities of 50,000+ until 26 April 2027 and smaller entities/special
  districts until 26 April 2028; the rule uses WCAG 2.1 AA and contains exceptions for qualifying
  archived and pre-existing content. This supports demand but does not imply every legacy PDF must
  be remediated. [DOJ compliance guide](https://www.ada.gov/resources/small-entity-compliance-guide/)
- **The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025** to covered products/services,
  including e-books. [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/eu-becomes-more-accessible-all-2025-07-31_en)
- **Canada's federal accessibility standards now explicitly cover digital documents.** The 2026
  guidance for regulated organizations includes PDF and Word documents under the adopted ICT
  standard. [Canada digital technologies guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/accessible-canada-regulations-guidance/category-digital-technologies.html)
- **Public purchasing is visible.** Washington's 2026 cooperative contract requires WCAG 2.2 AA
  and PDF/UA-1/2 across generic, STEM, legal, historical, translated, and interactive documents.
  Its award update lists 21 apparent successful vendors. That is evidence of demand **and**
  crowding. [Washington contract](https://apps.des.wa.gov/DESContracts/Home/ContractSummary/02024),
  [award update](https://apps.des.wa.gov/DESContracts/Home/ContractUpdates/02024)
- **AI traceability and human oversight now have regulatory and risk-management weight.** EU AI
  Act transparency rules began applying 2 August 2026; NIST's GenAI profile organizes work around
  govern, map, measure, and manage, including provenance, testing, and incident disclosure.
  [European Commission AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai),
  [NIST AI 600-1](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)
- **Records and archives require managed quality evidence.** NARA points agencies to FADGI 2023,
  quality-management guidance, transfer metadata, and contracting resources.
  [NARA digitization resources](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/policy/digitization)

### 5.2 Conditions that weaken a broad File Portal pitch

- **Conversion is crowded.** Docling is an MIT-licensed local conversion stack with a unified
  document representation and many formats. Azure Document Intelligence offers connected and some
  disconnected containers. ABBYY offers on-prem/private-cloud document processing. Local execution
  is valuable but is already an incumbent feature. [Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling),
  [Azure containers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/faq?view=doc-intel-4.0.0),
  [ABBYY Vantage architecture](https://www.abbyy.com/vantage/architecture/)
- **Remediation is crowded.** Washington's vendor roster, Adobe, Equidox, CommonLook/Allyant, and
  ABBYY all cover pieces of remediation, verification, or on-prem processing.
- **Organizations may stop publishing PDFs instead of remediating them.** Accessible HTML can be
  the better product and lower-cost path. This means File Portal must support disposition—remove,
  replace, archive, remediate, or human-review—not assume every input deserves a repaired PDF.
- **Local is not automatically required by privacy law.** Regulated cloud use can be lawful under
  defined controls. Local/private processing is a risk and deployment choice, not a blanket legal
  claim.
- **PDF/UA describes the accessible artifact, not how to repair it.** File Portal currently exports
  Markdown/library bundles and cannot claim PDF/UA, WCAG, EPUB Accessibility, FADGI, or NARA
  conformance. [PDF/UA-2 overview](https://pdfa.org/iso-14289-2-pdfua-2/)

### 5.3 Standards that should inform, not colonize, the design

| Standard/pattern | Useful concept | Do not overclaim |
|---|---|---|
| [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) | Entity, Activity, Agent, derivation, attribution | A provenance assertion is not proof that content is correct |
| [CloudEvents](https://cloudevents.io/) | Stable event ID/source/type/time/schema envelope | It does not provide delivery, durability, or business semantics |
| [W3C Trace Context](https://www.w3.org/TR/trace-context/) | Trace and parent identity across boundaries | HTTP headers are not required for a local file implementation |
| [C2PA](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.1/index.html) | Tamper-evident assertions and public content history | It explicitly does not decide whether an assertion is “good” |
| [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | Lifecycle governance, measurement, oversight | Voluntary risk guidance is not a product certification |
| PDF/UA / EN 301 549 / WCAG | Requirements for accessible outputs and interfaces | Automated checks alone do not establish human usability |

## 7. Competing implementation theses

### Thesis A — Additive trace/evidence spine

**Intent:** preserve filesystem queues and current owners; add a causal envelope and read-only trace
projection.

Proposed envelope:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "event_id": "uuid",
  "run_id": "uuid",
  "parent_run_id": null,
  "source_sha256": "full hash",
  "artifact_digest": "full hash or inventory digest",
  "producer": {"component": "...", "version": "..."},
  "occurred_at": "RFC3339 timestamp",
  "kind": "finding | policy_decision | authorization | transition | proof",
  "stage": "...",
  "data": {}
}
```

**Advantages:** smallest change; backward-compatible; exposes existing value; supports one-document
timeline; aligns with W3C PROV/CloudEvents concepts without importing a broker.

**Objections:** telemetry remains lossy if this is merely appended to current best-effort streams;
an envelope can standardize lies; adding IDs everywhere is cross-cutting; no buyer pays for a trace
unless it changes a decision or saves time.

### Thesis B — Authoritative per-document workflow kernel

**Intent:** make a versioned transition journal plus materialized state the authority for each
document generation, with explicit owners, idempotency keys, permitted transitions, and deletion
gates. Directories remain queue projections or are generated from the state machine.

**Advantages:** strongest consistency and recovery model; can reconcile retries and cross-machine
acknowledgements; makes policy mechanically testable.

**Objections:** high rewrite risk; duplicates reliable directory ownership already paid for in blood;
creates migration and crash-consistency problems; premature without multi-user scale or proven demand.

### Thesis C — Federated assurance exchange

**Intent:** package source identity, derivative hashes, tool/config, findings, human actions,
unresolved issues, and export proof into portable signed evidence bundles for organizations,
vendors, or multiple File Portal nodes.

**Advantages:** directly expresses the sketch's repeated grids and external endpoints; makes the
evidence layer a product; possible W3C PROV/C2PA/PREMIS crosswalks.

**Objections:** keys, identity, tenancy, privacy, retention, revocation, and interoperability become
product commitments; C2PA/PREMIS may not match the first buyer; no external party has asked for it.

### Thesis D — Accessibility remediation platform

**Intent:** turn conversion plus Repair Bench into a system that produces and validates PDF/UA,
accessible HTML, or EPUB.

**Advantages:** real regulated budgets and concrete standards; existing damage localization and
human workflow are relevant.

**Objections:** current output is not accessible PDF/EPUB; incumbents are mature; conformance and
assistive-technology testing are specialized disciplines; this risks mis-selling a quality-audit
system as a compliance product.

## 8. Recommended path: prove the wedge without a platform rewrite

### Stage 0 — Define the evaluable promise

Choose one claim only:

> “On a difficult document set, File Portal's exception/evidence layer finds more consequential
> silent damage or reduces expert review time versus the converter/remediation workflow alone.”

Pre-register the corpus, issue taxonomy, baseline tools, human reference process, metrics, and stop
condition. Do not use a market-size estimate as a substitute.

### Stage 1 — Read-only trace view

Build an adapter that reconstructs one document family from existing source SHA, manifests,
events, receipts, repairs, pending cards, and Git evidence. It must display missing links and
disagreement; it may not invent completion.

Acceptance:

- one fixture shows original conversion, retry, reanalysis, repair, supersede, and final receipt;
- every relationship names its evidence source;
- absent evidence renders `UNREAD`/unknown, never false absence;
- historical artifacts without new IDs remain readable.

### Stage 2 — Add causal IDs and typed gates

Generate `run_id` at admission. Propagate it and `parent_run_id` through every new durable artifact.
Keep five distinct record classes:

1. **finding** — what was measured;
2. **policy decision** — what consequence the rules selected;
3. **authorization** — who allowed an exception and why;
4. **transition proof** — what owner moved which identity between states;
5. **integrity proof** — what bytes/commit/inventory were checked.

Do not visually collapse `pass`, `allowed flag`, `human blessed`, `transport verified`, and `vault
committed` into one star.

### Stage 3 — Close the transport contract

Create a canonical sorted inventory of relative path, byte length, and SHA-256; hash the inventory;
verify it before the remote visible rename; return an acknowledgement bound to `run_id` and the
inventory digest. Test missing, extra, truncated, modified, retried, collision, ack-loss, and crash
cases.

### Stage 4 — Add the reader/reviewer outcome loop

Capture whether a finding was real, what correction was made, how long review took, whether the
derivative was useful, and whether the same signature should affect future audit/policy. Separate
training/calibration data from private source content. This is the loop that can prove product value.

### Stage 5 — Pick one vertical export only after evidence

- Accessibility: accessible HTML, PDF/UA, or EPUB plus manual assistive-technology evidence.
- Archives: preservation master separation plus PREMIS/METS/FADGI-aligned evidence.
- RAG: Markdown/JSON/chunks plus admission report and downstream answer evaluation.
- Legal: only after matter isolation, legal hold, redaction, and reviewer identity requirements.

Federation, signing, RBAC/SSO, multi-tenancy, and enterprise packaging follow a validated buyer; they
do not precede one.

## 9. What we know, know we do not know, and cannot learn from search

### 9.1 Known from current source and authoritative external evidence

- File Portal already preserves originals/anchors, derivatives, manifests, audit evidence, repairs,
  Git commits, and receipts in several layers.
- Its primary work queues are directories and atomic ownership transitions, not a message broker.
- Its event/receipt streams are valuable best-effort telemetry, not an authoritative event store.
- Human authority is architecturally separated from model advice and machine measurement.
- Cross-machine correlation, transport acknowledgement, projection freshness, and reader-outcome
  feedback are real gaps.
- Accessibility remediation, document QA, and AI traceability have current institutional demand.
- Generic conversion, remediation, and local/on-prem deployment are crowded capabilities.
- File Portal cannot currently claim accessibility, preservation, legal-chain-of-custody, or
  enterprise-security conformance.

### 9.2 Known unknowns

- Rab's intended legend, arrow direction, and star semantics.
- Deployed portal configuration, running processes, ThinkPad services, live queues, origin parity,
  and vault tip for this session.
- Cross-machine clock skew/order expectations, retention/classification policy, and event volume.
- The legal/organizational identity required for bless and repair authorization.
- The measurable false-positive/false-negative rate of the Survival Audit on a representative corpus.
- Which final format creates first-buyer value: HTML, PDF/UA, EPUB, Markdown/JSON, or preservation
  package.
- Whether the evidence layer reduces expert minutes enough to justify adoption.
- Support burden, deployment tolerance, budget owner, procurement path, and willingness to pay.

### 9.3 What web search cannot establish

- That any named organization wants File Portal, has an active budget, or will take a meeting.
- Independent truth of vendor accuracy, security, on-prem, or remediation claims.
- Private backlogs, failed pilots, security questionnaires, internal RFP attachments, and politics.
- Screen-reader user experience, archivist judgment, remediation specialist workflow, or legal
  defensibility without those people testing the artifact.
- File Portal conformance to WCAG, PDF/UA, EPUB Accessibility, FADGI, NARA, C2PA, or an evidentiary
  standard.
- Product-market fit, willingness to pay, switching cost, or the buyer's actual definition of
  “defensible.”
- The meaning of the hand-drawn symbols or which trade-offs Rab values.

### 9.4 The authoritative voices still missing

1. A remediation specialist who handles complex PDF/UA work.
2. A screen-reader user evaluating the final derivative, not an automated score.
3. An archivist/records officer defining masters, derivatives, metadata, retention, and authenticity.
4. A regulated RAG owner defining admission evidence and downstream failure cost.
5. A buyer/procurement/security owner defining deployment and support gates.
6. Rab's narrated diagram legend and product intent.

## 11. Final synthesis

The sketch's stars should not become generic “success” icons. They should become **typed claims**.
The repeated rows should not become a new cluster. They should remain pluggable fallible lanes. The
large central band should not become Kafka. It should become a causal evidence projection over the
filesystem truths already present. The triangle should remain where File Portal is strongest: an
explicit human exception and repair authority that machines cannot silently bypass.

The most promising File Portal is not the fastest converter. It is the system that can say:

> This immutable source entered here. These machines produced these derivatives under these
> versions and conditions. These exact passages or structures were at risk. This policy produced
> this consequence. This person accepted or repaired this bounded exception for this reason. These
> bytes arrived, this commit contains them, these issues remain unresolved, and this claim goes no
> further than the evidence.

That is already latent in File Portal. The next work is to connect it, test whether anyone values it,
and refuse to call the answer a market until real users and buyers supply the evidence web search
cannot.
