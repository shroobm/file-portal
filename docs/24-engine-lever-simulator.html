The Engine Lever — one capability, three scopes, and a choice card

Rab's chain, taken seriously: the models that can read a cropped zone can also read a page — and a whole PDF. That makes conversion a routing question, and this factory already has a law for routing questions: a lever the human clicks, where the position determines capability and level of output — exactly how analyst-mode.txt trades local privacy against Gemini speed today. Below: the investigation, then a simulator that replays the real timeline through the choice.

The scopes share everything that matters: the same reader, the same audit downstream, the same refusal posture, the same provenance. What changes is where the choice sits. At zone scope the choice is implicit (the Bench). At document scope it must be explicit — and the widget already owns the organ for explicit choices: the pre-flight card. Today that card asks "local or Gemini?" about the analyst. The investigation's conclusion is that the same card can ask "which eye?" about conversion — one more lever file, one more row of buttons, zero new laws.

Why this isn't feature creep: SYM-003 — table-loop degeneration — is the index's only OPEN row, and 4 of 4 table-or-figure-dense books through the GPU lane have hit it. The lever is not variety for its own sake; it is routing around the incumbent's one open disease, with the incumbent remaining the default for everything it is good at.
