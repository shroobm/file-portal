## §2 Against Marker alone

Marker alone gives a markdown file and a folder of figures. Everything in §1 B–E is absent: no fidelity number, no degeneration alarm, no rewrite gate, no verdict, no hold, no vault, no supersede, no bless, no fixity, no event stream, no glass, no bench, no receipts, no registers, no CI-observed close. §1 A adds the front door, the lane probe, the stall ladder and resume, and the two sidecars. **What Marker alone does that this factory does not change:** the OCR and layout quality of a single page — the engine is the engine (`marker-pdf 1.10.2`, Observed); this factory measures it, gates it, repairs it and records it; it does not improve a page Marker misread (§5).

## §3 Against the alternatives this project has measured

- **Chandra OCR 2 (Datalab):** designed as a bake-off (J25, register row 432) and **never run** — Historical, from the row: 5B params at BF16 do not fit the card's free memory (~10 GB vs 8,323 MiB free, Observed 2026-09-01), the licence is tighter than Marker's, Datalab's own olmOCR-bench figure (85.8 % vs Marker 76.5 %) is unverified here. **No measured comparison exists. This doc does not claim one.**
- **Gemini as the analyst backend:** a supported backend (`--backend gemini`) with its rates and quotas measured in the S6x sittings (segment-analyst; Historical). The corpus at 02:52Z names **`qwen3:8b` (local) in 21 of 33 manifests** and Gemini in none — the local backend is what the factory has run on since the analyst's near-exact gate exists. No head-to-head survival comparison local-vs-Gemini exists in the manifests read.
- **What IS measured, engine vs engine, is the factory's own two lanes on the same book:** the clean lane's text path and the scan lane's OCR path with an agreement witness — the scan lane's witness scored 0 of 1 pages on a four-word page (S175 E5) and the verdict said so.

## §4 Against the market, as the lane knows it (Inferred, unmeasured here)

The OCR-to-markdown products the lane knows of — Datalab's hosted Marker, Mistral OCR, Azure Document Intelligence, LlamaParse, IBM Docling, Reducto, Unstructured — compete on page-level accuracy, throughput and price. **Inferred:** none the lane knows of ships, as a product, (i) a per-book survival audit against its own pre-LLM reference with the denominators in the manifest, (ii) a degeneration tripwire that parks the book, (iii) a fail-closed supersede rail into a git vault with a human bless that refuses fails, or (iv) a glass detector that makes every produced number reach a surface or a signature. **Also Inferred:** several of them beat Marker 1.10 on hard scans and all of them beat this factory on throughput (one book at a time, 23–32 s/page Observed on this card). The competitive claim this doc can make with evidence is §1 B–E: **the audit, the gates, the vault rail and the observability are the moat; the engine is a commodity that can be swapped under them** (docs/61 modelled Marker as a pipeline tool for exactly this reason).

## §5 What is not yet good — so that §1 is credible

Rab asked for the good; the good is only believable beside the honest rest, one paragraph:

- **The engine's output on big books.** Of 11 distinct books, the best verdict is `pass` for **1** (Zero-to-One, after its bench repair), `flag` for 2, `fail` for 6, unaudited for 2; the degeneration tripwire is TRUE on 20 of 29 conversions that carry it — every conversion of both Damodaran editions, Equity Research and Diagnosing. Whether every one of those is a true repetition loop or the tripwire's false alarm on legitimate structure is the calibration question docs/15 §9 owns; the "zero-FP on the vaulted corpus" claim is Historical (S45) and has not been re-measured on this corpus. **Until it is, "faithfully restores" is proved on small books and DISPROVED-or-UNREAD on the large ones.**
- **The analyst gate fails 17 of 19.** At 0.995 the near-exact gate is strict by design; whether the analyst pass is worth its cost on a book it will fail is a lever question (`analyst-mode.txt`), not yet decided on numbers.
- **The visual half is unmeasured.** No artifact keeps the page images; `blocks.json` stays out of the vault; the twin proposal (S177, awaiting "twin") is the remedy.
- **The Dock's phase badge is source, not a running widget** (the rebuild + adoption are Rab's hand); the ViGEm device node likewise.
- **Throughput:** one book at a time on one card; 23–32 s/page on this night's pages; a 1,377-page book is hours.
