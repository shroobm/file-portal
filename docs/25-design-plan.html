PART·02

The research — what a keyboard reel actually is

Decomposed, the reels run on five ingredients: material mass (thick PBT keycaps, aluminum cases — surfaces that read as dense and matte, never glossy-cheap); damped acoustics — the "thock" is prized precisely because it is deep, foam-dampened, and restrained, not clacky; macro intimacy (one subject, shallow depth of field, everything else falls away); curation as identity (every component chosen, nothing default); and disciplined lighting (one warm accent under the caps — the tasteful builds are exactly NOT the RGB rainbow).

The thesis: the thock, not the RGB. File Portal already owns the hard part — a curated identity: terracotta on near-black, monospace, ISOTYPE glyphs, Beer's lineage. What it lacks is mass. Panels sit flat; state changes teleport; nothing has weight. The redesign is not a new face — it is giving the existing face physical presence: keycap-grade elevation, damped spring motion, one warm underglow, and a hero shot for the big screen.
