## §6 Deliberate Divergences

- **No URL-hash rewriting while scrolling** (many doc sites do): it fills back-button history
  with every chapter you scrolled past. Rejected knowingly.
- **Positional rule, not IntersectionObserver:** "current = last section whose top passed a
  reading line" is deterministic and matches how a reader actually says "I'm in chapter 14". IO's
  partial-visibility semantics add states without adding truth at this section size.
- **Classic `<script>`, not the existing module:** the mermaid CDN `await import` must never
  delay the spy; offline, the spy still runs while the diagrams degrade to text.
- **No widget rebuild** despite touching 8 `.rs` files: formatting has no behaviour; the rebuild
  ritual + adoption exist for behaviour. The exe Rab runs stays `F28C58A8`.
