## E1 — `analyst/rail-word-route-lexicon`: a label must be a word the book itself uses (19:0x–19:19Z)

**Built** (`windows-converter/table_geometry.py`, the lexicon route; `propose(lines, resolver, lex)`, `geometry_pass(text, resolver, use_lexicon=True)`): `lexicon(lines)` — every alphabetic token of three letters or more in the body, folded, with its count (the rail cells fall out by their own length; Valentine gives 7,522 / 7,528 words); `read_pattern(letters)` — the OCR's glyphs as what they could be: a Latin letter itself, a digit its confusion set (`6` → c, g, b — never any letter, which had let MOST fit `6OST` beside COSTS), an accented letter its base, a glyph with no base any letter; `letters_fit` tightened — a read must hold at least half its word's letters (`UE` is not CAUSE), a read of three letters or fewer must fit exactly after the doubled-letter collapse and may be at most one letter short of its word (`LLO` → LOW, never LABEL, never LOST); `fit_score` = letters placed − read letters unplaced − half the word's letters missed; `best_word(letters, lex, context)` — the one lexicon word with the best score, plus one point when the word occurs in the rows the rail labels (a group label echoes its rows' vocabulary: STRATEGY over STARTED for `SARTEGΫ`, whose rows ask how the strategy differs), the commonest on a tie, a tie within a quarter point refused as ambiguous; `boundary_collapse` — a plain Latin letter the OCR read twice across a row boundary is one letter (`L | L O` → LO; `VA | Ā` is left alone, the Ā being TI merged); `lexicon_segments(cell_letters, lex, context)` — a run of rail cells segmented into lexicon words at cell boundaries by a dynamic programme over the cells (a segment under `SEGMENT_MIN_SCORE` 1.25 refused; a segmentation scored by each word's EXCESS over the bar, so two weak words never beat one strong one; the rows' vocabulary chooses between words for the same cells and never inflates a segment's score; a fragment under three letters is no evidence unless it is the whole run); `apply_admitted` — a table's proposals admitted ONE AT A TIME, each checked by `grid_invariant` with those already kept (a single wrong label had refused the whole batch, caption and dots with it).
