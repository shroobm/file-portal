## §8 Decision Ledger

1. **The receiver keeps no soft clock.** `Observed` reasoning: MUSTER's value is that clock
   disagreement is mechanically detectable; a local mirror of the cookie tally would go stale on
   the next cookie and let MUSTER pass against a stale copy. **Rejected:** mirroring the desktop's
   TIME-STATE block (creates a second writer), and syncing it through the repo (turns a soft
   social record into a merge surface). Signed: Rab, in-session.
2. **Memory holds no counts.** Rejected: recording vault/held counts for convenience — that is
   precisely what rotted the 2026-07-25 note. Durable structure and pointers only; dated
   observations are permitted because past tense cannot lie about the present.
3. **Linux-side traps stay in the source.** Rejected: copying the pymupdf import-order and
   OCR-lane traps into memory. They already carry load-bearing comments in
   `converter/engines.py:7-12` and `converter/main.py:11-16`; a copy can drift, a pointer cannot.
4. **The contract is tiered 6/12 with an abort clause.** Rejected: all 18 sections mandatory —
   `CLAUDE_README.md:193` records close-outs already lost to power cuts, so an unconditional
   ceremony fails exactly when it matters. **This is the change most likely to be wrong** and the
   one to revisit first if the contract underperforms.
5. **Closeouts live in `sessions/`, ledger row becomes a pointer.** Rejected: inlining closeouts
   into the Change Ledger (would make the shared brain unreadable and trip the contract's own
   prose-mass anti-pattern on day one). `Intended` — **awaiting Rab's signature**, as it touches
   the shared brain the desktop also writes.
6. **The symptom index shipped seeded, not empty.** An empty index teaches nobody to read it.
