## §8 Decision Ledger
1. **Collapse removes noise, never claims restoration.** A collapsed zone reports
   `collapsed`, never `repaired`; the lost content still lives only in the page image.
2. **The excerpt is the durable anchor, the line number is the fragile one** (SYM-025). A
   live-body hit deliberately does NOT also take the drift terms — that would double-count.
3. **Collapse follows transcribe's discipline, not crop's** — undo push + a persisted SIGNED
   `delta` (not `lines`, which the UI renders as `r.lines || 0` and would corrupt on a
   negative). The old flat `+3` fallback in `_adjusted_line` would have mis-shifted every
   zone below a collapse; caught by research before it shipped.
4. **Runs are page-anchored, so they need no line arithmetic** — and where the witness text is
   mangled handwriting the anchor is honestly `None` rather than a guess.
5. **Recording precedes writing** in the chokepoint — the supersede ordering law generalised:
   an action whose intent cannot be filed must not happen.
6. **The ledger cannot forget a bypass.** Append-only + sha chain; `intact` stays false
   forever once something writes around the chokepoint.
7. **The rescore never blends measurement with judgment** (docs/28 §4). Degeneration clears;
   an omission does not clear because an image was placed. Eligibility is a recommendation
   routed to the bless rail, because `image-restored` and `dismissed-noise` are human
   assertions the machine must not credit itself.
8. **`dismissed-noise` requires a reason** — an unexplained dismissal is indistinguishable
   from ignoring the defect.
