## §8 Findings

1. 2. A stepper inside a drag handle must stop the handle's `pointerdown` or every tap starts a drag — the touch-then-move probe (80 px, nothing moves) is the tripwire.
3. A `transition` makes a computed value read as its start for the first frame — a `getComputedStyle` right after a change is not the resting value; read at rest, or wait the transition out.
