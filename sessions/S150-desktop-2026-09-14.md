**Captured BEFORE (E2, the whole of Valentine as it stands, one instrument, one CSV, one summary):** per table block — its lines, rows, cells per row, header/delimiter agreement, the health reading (the S149 rules, ported to Python), whether a GFM parser renders it and how many rows, the letter-column signature (column 1 mostly empty, its filled cells one to four letters or `<br>`-stacked letters, the letters concatenated top-down), header cells carrying `<br>`, the dot matrix (• per row, the total, the stray glyphs `٠ Ā Ė` and their like), the audit zone on it if any and its kind, the page it sits on where the block record knows it, and the analyst's chunk records that touched it (which classes were accepted or reverted there).
