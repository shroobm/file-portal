## E3 — the layer and the class built (17:05–17:23Z)

**The layer's repair half** (`windows-converter/table_geometry.py`, appended under the reading half): `fold_letters` / `letters_fit` — the word check that bounds the resolver (the word must contain the letters the OCR read, in order, but for ONE confusion and any number of missing letters; a doubled letter across a row boundary collapses: `SARTEGΫ` fits STRATEGY, `RlvĖNUE` fits REVENUE and not COSTS, `HGH` HIGH, `LLO` LOW, `6OSTs` COSTS, `VAĀON` VALUATION, `FNANČĹ` FINANCIAL); `propose(lines, resolver)` — per healthy table a `caption` (the spanning title, ≥ 20 characters with a space, its chopped tail `ment` dropped when it is the title's own suffix), a `rail` per letter run (the letters and the rows' own text to the resolver; the word on the run's first row, blanks under it; unresolved when there is no resolver, no word, a `?`, or a word that does not fit — every rail proposal carries its letters, its word and how), `dots` (stray glyphs → `•` inside a table that has a matrix); `apply_table` (an unchanged row keeps its bytes; with a caption the second row becomes the header and the delimiter row moves under it); `grid_invariant(before, after)` — the class's law on one table (one table on both sides; the same columns; the same rows but for a title lifted to a caption whose text sits above the table; every cell outside column 1 byte-identical or a stray glyph become `•`; column 1 changed only where a run became a fitting label with blanks under it) returning the reasons and the facts (cells compared, dots fixed, labels, caption); `geometry_pass(text, resolver)` — propose → apply on a copy → keep only what the invariant admits, the record written by the act (tables, with_signature, proposals, applied, refused, unresolved, labels, captions, unresolved_rails, refusals, dots_fixed, invariant_checks, resolver_calls).
