## §7 The Veto — measured first, thresholds second

Acceptance was fixed **before** any threshold was chosen: *p45 must veto, p84 must survive.*

| specimen | lines | words/line | text coverage | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IV p45 — shaded prose sidebar (false positive) | 32 | **9.25** | **0.731** | vetoed |
| Cyb p84 — Du Pont flow diagram (true positive) | 29 | **3.38** | **0.050** | survives |

**Line count does not discriminate** (32 vs 29) — the obvious metric is useless, which is
exactly why the thresholds had to be measured rather than guessed. Both conditions must hold to
veto, biasing toward keeping regions: a missed veto costs one false alarm, a wrong veto hides a
real lost figure, and those are not symmetrical.

Two more vetoes were forced by what the first one left behind:

- **TABLE veto** — the survivors were Damodaran's tables. On both prose metrics a table looks
  exactly like a diagram (p63: 4.76 words/line, coverage 0.319 — compare the diagram's 3.38 /
  0.050), so text shape *cannot* separate them. Structure can: MuPDF's `find_tables()` returns
  **1** on p63 and p73 and **0** on p84. Computed lazily, once per page, after every cheaper
  filter.
- **FRAME veto** — the "ILLUSTRATION N.N" boxes defeated both, because the *region* was wrong
  rather than the classification: clustering merges the frame border with its contents. Asking
  *is this region's area accounted for by text and tables?* catches the class without another
  threshold on text shape.

**Result across the corpus:** Investment Valuation 706 → 477 → 352 → **269** figure pages;
uncovered 625 → **309**. Cybernetics **still flags p84 at every stage** (68 → 57 pages, the
true positive intact). Scans unchanged (blind by construction). Cost 5.6 ms/page.

## §8 Where I Stopped, and Why

**p63 and p73 still survive all three vetoes.** Their text (0.319) plus table (~0.21) accounts
for 0.53 of the region against a 0.60 threshold. Lowering it to 0.50 would catch them — and
would be **tuning a threshold to a sample of two with no held-out set**. I stopped instead.
The real fix is clustering that does not let a frame border swallow its contents; that is a
different change and it needs its own calibration.

So the honest state: **706 → 269 is a 62 % cut in false alarms with the true positive
preserved**, and *the remaining 309 uncovered pages on that book are still unverified*. P-1 is
trustworthy on small born-digital books, improved-but-noisy on a large textbook, blind on scans.

## §9 A Limitation Found by Accident

Building a test fixture surfaced something the corpus had not: **zero-area paths are dropped
before clustering**. A pure horizontal or vertical connector line has no area, so it never joins
the boxes it connects — a flow diagram whose boxes sit further apart than the cluster gap can
fragment into sub-threshold pieces and be **missed entirely**. A false-*negative* path, in the
instrument built to find false negatives. Recorded in the module header; not fixed.

The fixtures themselves failed twice for reasons unrelated to vetoing (single-path shapes
filtered before the veto ran; then the connector problem above). After the second, the veto
cases were rewritten to unit-test the discriminator against the **real measured numbers**, with
end-to-end behaviour verified by the corpus run instead. Synthetic fixtures more delicate than
the thing under test are not evidence.
