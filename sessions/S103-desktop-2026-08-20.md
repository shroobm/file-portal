## §7 Decision Ledger

- **Both halves of his sentence were already doctrine, and both had failed as doctrine.** fmt
  was prescribed FIRST in docs/19:68 with SYM-020's reasoning attached; S101 skipped it anyway.
  So the build is mechanical, not more prose — the guard runs the check rather than reminding
  someone to.
- **UNREAD never blocks and never claims clean.** A close on a plane must still be closeable; a
  close that dies when GitHub is unreachable teaches people to skip the close, which is how the
  ritual would rot in the same way the ritual it replaces did.
- **Only a MEASURED red exits 1.** "In progress" is explicitly not a green — it prints a
  CHECK IT AFTER instruction instead of a verdict it does not have.
- The `--enforce` fix was **not** in his commission and was fixed anyway: the close contract was
  prescribing the exact form SYM-046 proved meaningless, in two places.
