## §5 The design, before it is built

**Why this is not simply "time the loop."** S80 deliberately did not measure speed, because a
single wall-clock number conflates four different costs: model load, prompt processing, token
generation, and transport. S79's `+27 %` is unbankable partly because nobody can now say which of
those four it described. The arm therefore reports the components separately, using the names the
field already uses, and refuses to print a rate whose denominator it did not observe.

**The vocabulary is the deliverable, not a by-product.** `docs/34` fixes it; the harness is its
first consumer and prints in it; the manual carries it for the operator. If the three ever
disagree, the harness is wrong — a document may describe an instrument, but the instrument may
not invent a word the document does not define.

**What "matches the world" means concretely:** prefill/decode split as in llama.cpp's `pp`/`tg`
and Ollama's `prompt_eval_*`/`eval_*`; TTFT and TPOT as in the serving literature; p50/p95 rather
than a bare mean; cold and warm never mixed in one number; a speedup always printed as the two
measurements it divides.
