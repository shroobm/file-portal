## §3 The open incident, and its reconcile

`open.sh` exited **1** on its first run of the session:

```
[2] SOFT clock ... ✗ DRIFT tally=84 hook= (hook read within 6 lines of TIME-STATE)
```

`Observed` — the two records were read directly and **agree in substance**:

- `cookie-tally.md:13` — `**Received from user: 84**`
- `MEMORY.md` TIME-STATE — `cookies **received 84 / given 3 …**`

The `hook=` field came back **empty**, not wrong. `muster.sh:75` matches
`received [0-9]+ / given [0-9]+` line-wise over the TIME-STATE block, and the S113 close had
re-wrapped that sentence so the phrase straddled a newline — line 11 ended `cookies **received`
and line 12 began `84 / given 3`. A line-wise regex cannot see across that break.

**Verdict: CONFIG fault. Not a rewind, not a fork, not a blind session.** The HARD clock was
`✓` throughout (`14a526b`, ledger-equal, ancestor of HEAD), so only the soft parse was
implicated, and the substance of both clocks matched.

**Reconcile:** the TIME-STATE sentence was re-wrapped so `received 84 / given 3` sits
contiguous on one line. No count changed; no content moved out of `MEMORY.md` (S96's ruling).
Re-running `open.sh` returned `[2] SOFT clock ... ✓ 84 (tally == hook)` and exit 0.

**Filed as a defect, because a guard that a re-wrap can defeat is a guard with a silent
failure mode**: the same edit that made this fire *loudly* could have made a REAL drift read as
a benign empty parse in the other direction. See SYM-068.
