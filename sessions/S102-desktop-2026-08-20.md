**And the markdown shows what became of it: the diagram's own labels are sitting in the prose**
— `MTM Venture Committees`, `Squeeze all areas to lower costs`, `Teach the World`,
`Customer Conversation`, `Improve Internal Productivity` — with **zero image embeds** anywhere
in that window. The figure was not lost; it was **transmuted into text**.

That is **the #598 hole, live in Rab's own library**, and it is precisely the failure docs/40
predicted both existing gates cannot see: it produces *more* text, not less, so degeneration

of the one class nothing else in the world detects.** The instrument justified itself on its
first run.

### §9.2 The honest ceiling: P-1 is BLIND on the scan lane

Both scan-lane books report `figpp 0 / coverage None`. This is correct behaviour, not a bug:
on a scan every page IS one full-page image, excluded by the `max_page_fraction` filter (which
must exist, or every scanned page would flag). But it means **P-1 says nothing whatsoever about
scanned books** — and the scan lane is the harder, more failure-prone half of this corpus.
Finding figures inside a page raster needs layout analysis, not object enumeration; that is a
different instrument and it is not built. Stated plainly rather than buried, because a
coverage report that silently returns `None` for half the library could easily be misread as
"all clear".
